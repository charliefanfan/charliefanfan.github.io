How LovePact handles your data

You can use local features without an account. If you sign in and pair, shared content is stored with Supabase so your partner can access it. Apple processes Pro subscriptions.

Local use

Your local tasks, lists, ledger, preferences, Pro shopping templates and recurrence settings are saved on your device. If you enable a supported device backup, Apple’s backup settings determine what is included.

Accounts and pairing

Supabase Auth processes the email address and password you enter to create or access your account. LovePact stores session credentials in the device’s Keychain. We use account identifiers and pairing codes to authenticate users and restrict shared data to their pairing. Share your invitation code only with the person you want to pair with.

Shared content and photos

Shared tasks, comments, shopping lists, wishes, point activity, and penalty and settlement records are sent to Supabase to synchronize across paired devices. Your paired partner can view the content in your shared space. If you choose a proof photo with the system photo picker, the app receives the selected photo. For shared tasks, submitting proof uploads a copy for review. For local tasks, proof stays on your device unless you share it yourself.

Purchases and service providers

Apple processes payments, renewals, refunds and Family Sharing under its own policies. LovePact checks your StoreKit subscription status to enable Pro features and does not receive your card number. Cash penalty entries are bookkeeping only. LovePact does not transfer money between partners.

Supabase provides authentication and hosting. Hosting and authentication services can process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to operate and secure their services. LovePact does not include advertising or use your shared content for targeted advertising.

Retention and deletion

Shared content is retained while the shared space exists. In the account screen, use Unpair to remove the shared space, or Delete Account to remove your LovePact account and its shared space. Either action can remove shared records for both partners. Deleting the app alone does not delete your server account. Backups or service logs may persist temporarily. Apple subscription cancellation is managed separately in Apple’s subscription settings.

Choices and contact

Use the app to manage your content or delete your account. For privacy questions or requests to access, correct or delete information, email charliefanfan84@gmail.com. We may need to verify your account before responding. This policy may change as LovePact changes. The date above identifies the current version.

LovePact 隱私權政策

本機功能可免登入使用。登入並配對後，共享內容會儲存在 Supabase，讓伴侶能同步查看。Pro 訂閱由 Apple 處理。

本機使用

本機的任務、清單、帳本、偏好設定、Pro 採買範本和週期設定儲存在裝置上。若你開啟支援的裝置備份，Apple 的備份設定會決定哪些資料會納入備份。

帳號與配對

Supabase Auth 會處理你建立帳號或登入時輸入的電子郵件和密碼。LovePact 將登入憑證儲存在裝置的鑰匙圈。我們使用帳號識別碼和邀請碼來驗證使用者，並限制共享資料只能由配對成員存取。請只將邀請碼分享給你想配對的人。

共享內容與照片

共享任務、留言、採買清單、願望、點數活動、罰款和結清紀錄會送到 Supabase，以便在配對裝置之間同步。你的伴侶可以查看共享空間內的內容。透過系統照片選擇器加入證明時，App 只取得你選的照片。提交共享任務的證明時，照片副本會上傳供審核。本機任務的證明留在裝置上，除非你自行分享。

購買與服務供應商

付款、續訂、退款和家人共享由 Apple 依其政策處理。LovePact 透過 StoreKit 確認訂閱狀態以啟用 Pro 功能，不會取得你的卡號。現金罰款功能只記錄款項，LovePact 不會在伴侶之間代收或轉帳。

Supabase 提供身分驗證與託管。供應商可能為了維護服務和安全而處理 IP 位址、請求日誌等技術資訊。LovePact 沒有廣告功能，也不會把你的共享內容用於定向廣告。

保存與刪除

共享資料會在共享空間存在期間保留。在帳號畫面，你可以解除配對以移除共享空間，或刪除帳號以移除 LovePact 帳號及其共享空間。兩種操作都可能移除雙方的共享紀錄。僅刪除 App 不會刪除伺服器帳號，備份或服務日誌可能暫時保留。Apple 訂閱需在 Apple 的訂閱設定中另行取消。

你的選擇與聯絡方式

你可以在 App 管理內容或刪除帳號。若要詢問隱私權、查閱、更正或刪除資料，請寄信至 charliefanfan84@gmail.com。我們可能需要先驗證帳號才能回覆。LovePact 功能變更時，本政策也可能更新，請以頁首日期為準。
