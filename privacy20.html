Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 5, 2026

Welcome to Pawcus: Dog Focus Timer ("the App"). We are committed to protecting your personal privacy. This policy explains how we handle information related to your personal data.

1. Data Collection and Storage

We believe privacy is a fundamental human right. Therefore, the App is designed with a "Local Data" principle:

  • Personally Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any data that can be used to identify you.
  • Focus Data: Your focus session records, task names, dog levels, and XP progress are stored directly on your local device.
  • iCloud Sync: If you enable iCloud features, relevant data will be encrypted and transmitted to your personal iCloud account. We have no access to your iCloud storage content.

2. How We Use Data

All data processed by the App is used exclusively for core functionalities:

  • Tracking and displaying your focus progress.
  • Managing your dog’s growth and unlocking skins.
  • Generating personalized activity statistics and charts.

3. Third-Party Services and Sharing

We do not share, sell, or rent your personal data to any third-party organizations. The App does not contain any third-party advertising trackers or analytics SDKs (such as AdMob or Facebook SDK).

4. User Control

You have full control over your data:

  • You can edit or delete task records within the App at any time.
  • Deleting the App will remove all data stored on the local device.
  • You can manage or disable iCloud synchronization at any time through the iOS System Settings.

5. Information Security

Since data is stored locally on your device and within the secure iCloud environment provided by Apple, its security is protected by iOS system security and Apple’s privacy framework.
